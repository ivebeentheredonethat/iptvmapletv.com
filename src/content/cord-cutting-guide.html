Cutting the cord means cancelling your cable or satellite TV and watching through the internet instead. It is one of the few household bills you can shrink in an afternoon. This guide walks through why people do it, what you need and how to switch with no gap in your viewing.

Why switch from cable?

  • Cost. Cable packages bundle channels you never watch, plus box rentals and fees.
  • Flexibility. Cable stays in one room. Internet TV follows you to the bedroom, the cottage or a trip.
  • Choice. You pick the app and the screens instead of the package the provider defines.
  • No contract. Most internet TV plans can be stopped when you want.

How to switch in four steps

  1. Test first. Start the free 24-hour trial. It needs no credit card, just your WhatsApp or email so we can send the login.
  2. Set up one TV. Install the app and follow the beginner guide. It takes about ten minutes.
  3. Watch a live game and a movie on your own internet to see that it runs well.
  4. Only then cancel cable. Keep the old service until the new one has proven itself for a week or two.
